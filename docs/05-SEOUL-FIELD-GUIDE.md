# 05 · Seoul field guide and content plan
Researched 7 September 2026. This is practical setup guidance for the app, not a date-specific itinerary or a guarantee of services. Exact travel dates and the accommodation are unknown. Sources are linked in `SOURCES.md`.

## The transit setup to make before leaving
Install Naver Maps on every traveler iPhone and test a search using a Korean destination name. Let Naver handle live directions; store your chosen places, Korean names and station exits in Seoul Pocket. A saved route screenshot is a fallback reference, not proof that a train is still running or a service disruption has cleared [S7, S21].

**Digital Tmoney is now a genuine option to check.** The current MobileTmoney publisher listing offers an international-user/no-sign-up flow with Apple Pay top-up and currently names Mastercard, American Express, UnionPay and JCB. Visa is not listed in those current notes. Check the exact device, OS, card and region; complete a small successful top-up rather than assuming every Apple Pay card works. This is a prepaid Tmoney card on the phone, not universal direct acceptance of a foreign bank card at every transit gate [S16].

For a low-complexity fallback, each traveler can carry a physical transit card and some KRW cash for the purchase/top-up options available at the point of sale [S17]. Seoul's published open-loop bank-card rollout is phased; it should not be interpreted as proof of universal coverage on your travel dates [S19]. Old advice that "iPhones cannot use Tmoney" and broad advice that "just tap any Apple Pay card" are both poor foundations for this app.

## How to use the subway and buses
Choose your destination, then follow the line and direction, not merely the station name. Check the relevant exit because it affects the final walk. Keep the same transit card for the journey and transfers. Tap entering and leaving subway gates; tap on boarding and getting off buses. Keep the exact destination in Korean available to show someone when directions become confusing [S20].

Save the closest useful station/exit to the accommodation, but verify that it matches the actual building entrance. Accessibility routes and lifts should be checked separately when relevant. Before a late evening out, check the final viable route home and keep a taxi/address fallback. The app deliberately does not invent a universal last-train time [S21].

## Which pass should you buy?
Do not hardcode a pass recommendation before the dates and intended routes are known. Seoul's Climate Card and tourist-oriented options have specific coverage and validity rules. Official pages currently contain date-dependent changes, including upcoming changes discussed for October 2026, so an old airport/pass summary can be misleading. Check current official coverage for both the outward and return journey, especially airports and travel outside the core area [S17–S18].

The app should store a short personal decision: which card each person will use, where they will obtain/top it up, the last confirmed rules date and the official source. It should not store payment-card numbers or promise that a pass covers a route it has not checked.

## Arrival and getting home
Save flight/terminal, the Korean accommodation address, check-in instructions, host contact and the selected airport transfer before flying. AREX Express and All Stop are different services; confirm your intended product and final connection on the official site rather than treating them interchangeably [S22]. Station names, screenshots and a driver card are useful even when the online route is unavailable.

In Seoul Pocket, keep room-entry instructions in the encrypted stay vault. Put only a non-sensitive version of the address and emergency contacts on the optional printed fallback. Do not put the building PIN into Naver search or a publicly shared itinerary link.

## Emergency and language preparation
In Korea, 112 is police and 119 is ambulance/fire. The 1330 tourist helpline provides travel information and language assistance; consult its current site for service hours and online options rather than assuming every language is staffed continuously [S23]. A phone number in an offline app does not make a call work without a usable calling connection. Check what the actual travel SIM/phone arrangement supports before leaving.

The app includes conventional everyday phrase drafts and approximate phonetics. They are useful starting content, not native-reviewed medical translation. For any serious allergy, ask for a professionally or competently reviewed custom card stating the specific allergen and relevant cross-contact concern; do not rely on a generic "not spicy" phrase to establish ingredients or safety. Keep that card offline and show the Korean text.

## Food and destinations: structure, not an invented ranking
The starter offers opt-in ideas such as Gyeongbokgung, a market visit and familiar Korean dishes. These are prompts for a personal shortlist, not endorsements of specific vendors or a current top-ten ranking [S25]. Each real saved venue should gain a Korean name, road address, source link, neighborhood and a date when details were checked. A dish idea can remain unscheduled until a venue is chosen.

A useful planning method is one anchor activity plus one food objective in a neighborhood, with another place as an optional extension. An example planning cluster could pair a palace-area visit with nearby food research, or a market visit with a dish checklist. These are illustrative planning approaches, not checked walking routes or confirmed opening-day recommendations. Avoid imposing a packed schedule before the traveler has supplied interests and dates.

Before committing to a venue, check operating day, last admission/order, reservation requirement, address, queue policy and the actual menu needed by the group. Social content can be old or omit the venue name; keep the reel URL plus your own note explaining why it was saved. Add a screenshot or local cover photo only when you have permission to retain it. Seoul Pocket does not cache the social video.

## Currency use on the street
Use the saved-rate converter for a quick price estimate in either direction. It labels when the reference was saved; a manually entered rate remains clearly manual. A provider rate is not a live quote from the business, an exchange booth or the user's card issuer [S15]. Before travel, save a reference once and test conversion with no internet. Do not remove a functioning offline rate just because a refresh fails.

## Useful additions for this particular app
An airport arrival card, confirmed-reservation cards, neighborhood grouping, an indoor alternative for each day, favorite phrases, an explicit predeparture checklist and a separate copy of essential contact information are the highest-value next additions. A weather feed can help but must be optional; a failed forecast cannot hide the itinerary. Shared expenses and automatic itinerary suggestions can wait until storage, sync and recovery are proven.
