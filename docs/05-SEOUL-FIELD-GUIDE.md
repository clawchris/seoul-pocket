## Transit essentials retained
Use live Naver routing for the exact journey and keep the Korean destination available [S7,S21]. Tap in/out at subway gates and when boarding/leaving buses so the fare/transfer process can work correctly [S20]. Before a late return, check the actual last route and retain the stay address as a fallback. Airport route selection depends on your real terminal/time; the checklist asks you to confirm it rather than inventing a transfer.

Do not treat an iPhone Tmoney card and direct use of an ordinary overseas bank card at a gate as the same thing. Test the current MobileTmoney/Apple Wallet flow on the actual phone and payment card; provider support changes [S16]. Seoul's direct bank-card gate rollout is phased [S19]. Keep a tested physical-card/cash alternative when the digital flow is not proven. Climate Card suitability/coverage depends on actual routes and dates; no pass purchase is preselected [S17–S18]. AREX Express and All Stop are distinct services; confirm the correct ticket/connection with AREX [S22].

## Weather and time
The app requests an approximate Gwangjin forecast, not the exact stay or live GPS. It retains a dated last-successful copy, but an offline forecast does not update. Current conditions are model estimates, not guaranteed live observation or official warnings [S37]. Compare the forecast to the date of the outing, especially after returning from offline use. No unknown future trip dates have been assigned a forecast.

Seoul, Singapore and Cupertino are the only comparison cities. Cupertino's displayed date can differ from Seoul's. The app calculates via the phone's timezone data and marks PDT/PST rather than assuming one fixed offset [S39]. Keep the phone's automatic time enabled. This is a conversion tool, not an event scheduling system.
